# Cosmixify — Site Guide

A friendly guide to everything you can do on Cosmixify. No technical knowledge required.

---

## Welcome to Cosmixify

**Cosmixify** is a space-themed website where you can explore stunning astronomy photos, daily images from NASA, upcoming sky events, and photos shared by fellow stargazers.

Our mission is simple: bring the wonders of the cosmos closer to everyone — whether you are a curious beginner or a passionate astronomy fan.

---

## Who Can Use the Site?

### Visitors (no account needed)

You can browse photos, search the gallery, read about sky events, use the calendar, try fun tools, and explore the solar system — all without signing up.

### Registered members

Create a free account to:

- Save your favorite photos
- Upload your own astrophotography
- Manage your profile and see the status of your submissions

### Site administrators

A separate admin area is available for the team that runs Cosmixify. They manage photos, events, members, and community uploads. See the [Admin Panel](#admin-panel-for-site-staff) section below.

## Homepage Tour

When you open Cosmixify, here is what you will see from top to bottom.

### Welcome popup

A short greeting may appear when you first arrive. Click the button to start exploring.

### Hero section

The main welcome area introduces Cosmixify with a friendly message and two buttons:

- **Explore photos** — scrolls down to the latest gallery
- **View calendar** — opens the astronomical events calendar

### Latest photos

A hand-picked selection of rare and beautiful space images from our main gallery — nebulae, galaxies, planets, and more. Tap any image to see the full picture and details. Use **View all** to see the complete latest collection.

### Upcoming events

A preview of sky events coming soon — meteor showers, planet viewing nights, and similar happenings. Each card shows the date and a short description. Tap **More details** for full viewing tips, or **Full calendar** to see the entire month.

### NASA images

Daily space pictures from **NASA’s Astronomy Picture of the Day (APOD)**. These update regularly with real images and explanations from NASA. Tap any card to read more, or **View all** for the full list.

### Top rated

The most popular photos in the gallery — ranked by how many people have viewed them. A great place to discover crowd favorites.

### Community

Photos uploaded by Cosmixify members. Only approved submissions appear here. If the section is empty, you could be the first to share your work.

### Categories

Browse photos by topic:

| Category | What you will find |
|----------|-------------------|
| Galaxies | Distant galaxies and cosmic structures |
| Planets | Planets in our solar system |
| James Webb | Images from the James Webb Space Telescope |
| Stars | Stars, constellations, and night-sky wonders |
| NASA | Official NASA imagery |
| ESA | Images from the European Space Agency |

Tap a category to search the gallery for related photos.

### Solar system

An interactive section at the bottom of the homepage where you can explore the planets of our solar system in a visual, engaging way.

## Photo Galleries

Cosmixify has several gallery pages. All of them use the same card layout — tap any image to open its detail page.

| Page | What it shows |
|------|----------------|
| Latest (`last.php`) | All recent gallery photos |
| Top rated (`TopRated.php`) | Gallery photos sorted by popularity |
| All NASA photos (`all-nasa-photos.php`) | The full NASA daily image collection |
| Community photos (`all_user_photos.php`) | All approved member uploads |
| By tag (`LookingFor.php`) | Photos matching a specific tag |

### Photo detail pages

When you open a photo, you will see:

- **Title and description** — what the image shows and why it matters
- **Category** — e.g. Nebulae, Planets, Galaxies
- **Source** — where the image comes from, with a link to learn more
- **Date** — when it was added or captured
- **Tags** — tap a tag to find similar photos
- **Related photos** — more images from the same category

**If you are logged in**, you can also:

- **Add to favorites** — save the photo to your profile
- **Share** — copy a link to send to friends
- **Download** — save the image to your device

There are three types of detail pages:

- **Gallery photos** — curated space images from NASA and partner agencies
- **NASA daily photos** — images from NASA’s picture of the day
- **Community photos** — images shared by members

## Astronomical Events

### Calendar

The **event calendar** shows the current month day by day. Days with an active sky event are highlighted. Tap a day to see the event name and open its detail page.

### Event details

Each event page includes:

- **Event name** — e.g. Perseid Meteor Shower, Saturn at Opposition
- **Start and end dates** — when the event is active
- **Description** — what to expect and when to look up
- **Best viewing method** — naked eye, binoculars, or telescope
- **Light pollution** — whether you can see it from a city or need darker skies
- **Best viewing location** — which part of the world or sky works best

**Helpful extras:**

- **Add a Google Calendar reminder** — so you do not miss the event
- **Light pollution map** — an interactive map to find darker skies near you

## Fun Tools

### Space age calculator

Visit the **space age calculator** and enter your birthday. The site shows how old you would be on other planets — Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, and more — based on how long each planet takes to orbit the Sun. A fun way to see time differently across the solar system.

## Community Sharing

Love astrophotography? Members can share their own work with the Cosmixify community.

### How to upload

1. **Sign in** or create an account
2. Go to **Share** from the homepage or your profile
3. Fill in:
   - Photo title
   - Description
   - Date the photo was taken
   - Camera or device used
4. Select your image file and submit

### What happens next

Your photo goes into a **review queue**. The Cosmixify team checks it before it appears publicly. You can track the status in your profile:

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Pending | Waiting for review |
| Approved | Live on the site for everyone to see |
| Rejected | Not published (may not meet guidelines) |

## Where Do the Images Come From?

Cosmixify is built around **real space content**:

- The main gallery features images from **NASA**, **ESA**, the **Hubble Space Telescope**, **James Webb**, and other trusted space missions
- The NASA section pulls from **NASA’s official Astronomy Picture of the Day**
- Sky events are based on **real astronomical dates** — meteor showers, planet oppositions, and seasonal viewing opportunities
- Community photos come from **members like you**, reviewed before publication

The site is kept fresh with new images and upcoming events so there is always something new to discover.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I find a specific photo?**  
Use the search bar at the top. Try words like planet names, *nebula*, *galaxy*, or *NASA*. You can also browse by category from the homepage.

**How do I save a photo I like?**  
Create an account and log in. Open the photo’s detail page and tap **Add to favorites**. Find all saved photos in your profile.

**How do I share my own astrophoto?**  
Log in, go to **Share**, fill in the details, upload your image, and submit. Check your profile later for approval status.

**How do I know when to watch the sky?**  
Open the **calendar** from the homepage or the hero section. Browse upcoming events, read the details, and optionally add a reminder to your Google Calendar.

**Can I see events if I live in a city with bright lights?**  
Each event page says whether it is visible from light-polluted areas. For darker skies, use the **light pollution map** linked from event details.

**How do I switch language?**  
On the homepage, use the language button at the top right (or top left in Arabic). The site will switch between English and Arabic.

**Do I need an account to use Cosmixify?**  
No. Browsing, searching, viewing events, and using the space age calculator are all free without signing up. An account is only needed for favorites and uploads.

**Is Cosmixify free?**  
Yes. Exploring the site and creating an account are free.
